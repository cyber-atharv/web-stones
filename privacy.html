Privacy Policy.

Last Updated & Verified: October 2026 • Compliant with DPDP Act (India) & Global Privacy Standards

1. Operational Commitment

Murali Granites & Marbles ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of homeowners, architects, interior designers, and commercial builders who visit our digital showroom, engage with our 3D room visualizer, or submit quotation inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information necessary to provide accurate stone quotations, schedule physical stockyard visits, and coordinate slab deliveries across India:

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone/WhatsApp number, and email address provided when requesting quotations or communicating via our WhatsApp hotline.
  • Project Specifications: Stone variety preferences (e.g., River Black, Statuario Marble), room applications (kitchen countertop, living room flooring, facade), approximate square footage, and delivery location/city.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: Non-personally identifiable usage statistics, device types, screen resolutions, and visualizer interaction metrics collected via Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to optimize website performance.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

Your information is collected and processed exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To calculate accurate per-square-foot quotation estimates based on current lot thickness and finish.
  • To verify available slab lots at our Bargur, Krishnagiri stockyard and provide high-resolution photos/videos of exact bundles.
  • To coordinate A-frame wooden crate logistics and site delivery dispatch.
  • To answer architectural and technical inquiries regarding ASTM stone diagnostics and maintenance.

4. Third-Party Disclosures & Data Transfers

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize client personal data under any circumstances. Information is shared only with trusted logistics operators strictly for transit dispatch, or when required by applicable Indian law.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes strictly necessary cookies for navigation and aggregated analytics tokens (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity). You can configure your browser to reject cookies; core stone browsing and visualizer features will remain fully functional.

6. Data Retention & Security Measures

Client inquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to complete commercial transactions and fulfill tax, accounting, or warranty obligations. All records are guarded with access controls, and administrative routes are protected against unauthorized inspection.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection regulations (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated contact information.
  • Request deletion or erasure of your quotation records from our communications database.
  • Opt out of direct promotional updates at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing us.
