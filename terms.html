Architectural Supply Standards

Terms of Supply.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Natural Stone Trade & Quarry Logistics Standards

1. Natural Stone Inherent Characteristics

Granite, quartzite, and marble are authentic geological formations quarried from the earth. Variations in background shade, vein movement, mica speckles, and natural quartz crystals are intrinsic hallmarks of genuine stone, not manufacturing defects.

2. Physical Slab Lot Hand-Inspection

Homeowners, architects, and site engineers are welcome and encouraged to physically inspect and tag full bundles under natural daylight at our Bargur, Krishnagiri stockyard prior to billing and dispatch. For remote clients across South India, we provide uncompressed, high-definition video walkthroughs of the exact numbered slabs allocated to your project.

3. Calibration & Thickness Tolerances

Our Krishnagiri manufacturing facility uses automated multi-blade gang saws and diamond-line polishing machines. Standard calibrated slab thicknesses (16mm, 18mm, 20mm, 30mm) follow international ASTM standards with a manufacturing tolerance of ±1mm.

4. Quotations & Price Validity

  • All rate estimates provided per square foot (Sq.Ft) are based on current gang-saw lot inventory and are valid for 15 calendar days from issuance.
  • Quotations exclude local state GST and unloading crane charges unless explicitly detailed in an official pro-forma invoice.
  • Orders are confirmed upon advance booking deposit and allocation of specific slab bundle numbers.

5. Packaging & Wooden A-Frame Crating

To prevent hairline stress cracks and transit chipping, all outstation dispatches are secured in reinforced heavy-duty wooden A-frame crates with shock-absorbent corner guards and plastic weather wrapping. Slabs are loaded and balanced using overhead gantry cranes.

6. Transit Delivery & Unloading Inspection

  • Upon arrival of the transport vehicle at the project site, the client or authorized site contractor must inspect the wooden crates before unloading.
  • Any transit breakage or structural fracture must be documented with clear unboxing photographs and endorsed on the lorry receipt (LR) copy within 24 hours of arrival.
  • Murali Granites & Marbles will replace verified transit-damaged slabs from matched inventory lots.

7. Cutting, Fabrication & Installation Care

Once slabs are cut, bullnosed, or installed by the client's local stone masons, we cannot accept returns or claims regarding edge chips or breakage caused by improper substrate mortar, hollow grouting, or dry-cutting blades. We recommend wet-cutting diamond blades and calibrated adhesive application.
