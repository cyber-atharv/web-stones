About Murali 3D Showroom

Welcome to the Murali Granites & Marbles 3D Architectural Showroom. This interactive experience allows architects, interior designers, and homeowners to preview luxury natural stone slabs, imported marble flooring, and custom tile grids inside a real-time living space.

Featuring physically-based materials (PBR) with calibrated anti-glare finishes, you can inspect authentic marble veining, compare honed satin versus polished gloss finishes, and test multiple flooring and wall formats in real time.
