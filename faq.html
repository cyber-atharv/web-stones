Yes, we welcome architects, interior designers, and homeowners to visit our stockyard showroom to inspect and select specific stone blocks or slab sequences. We highly recommend booking an appointment beforehand so our stone experts can guide you.

We accept secure wire transfers (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS), credit/debit card payments, UPI transfers, and demand drafts. For international commercial orders, Letter of Credit (L/C) arrangements can also be processed.

Standard delivery within local and neighboring regions takes 3 to 5 business days. National deliveries across India range between 7 to 14 days depending on transit distance and quarry availability for custom bulk orders.

We do not perform installations directly, but we provide detailed installation guidelines (substrate preparation, adhesive selection, sealing specifications, and vein matching diagrams) and can recommend verified professional stonemasons in your area.
