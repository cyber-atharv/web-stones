Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about slab inspection, quarry direct logistics, and custom cutting.

Can I inspect the physical slab lots before booking? +
Yes, absolutely. We welcome homeowners and architects to our Bargur, Krishnagiri stockyard to hand-inspect full bundles under natural daylight. We also provide high-resolution uncompressed video walkthroughs of your specific lots before dispatch.
What is the difference between granite and marble durability? +
Granite is an igneous stone formed under volcanic heat, making it practically scratch-proof and impervious to lemon acids, ideal for kitchen countertops. Marble is metamorphic calcium carbonate, prized for luminous veining in floors, master vanities, and feature walls.
What are your delivery timelines across South India? +
In-stock slabs are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours from Krishnagiri. Transit times are typically same-day/next-day to Bangalore, Chennai, and Salem, and 2-3 days for Kerala, Hyderabad, and Andhra Pradesh sites.
Do you supply custom cut-to-size slabs and step treads? +
Yes. In addition to full jumbo gang-saw slabs, our Krishnagiri facility houses CNC bridge saws capable of precision edge chamfering, full bullnosing, undermount sink cutouts, and custom staircase tread fabrication.
